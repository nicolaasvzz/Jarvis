Is the data on point? Yes, and here's what it shows

  • The start was a disaster (−24% a year), and fixing it was real progress: version 1 is positive on markets it never saw.
  • Version 2's gains were fitted to the 21 training markets. On them, version 1's shorts made +0.32R a trade and its longs lost −0.10R, so "shorts only" looked great. On the 24 untouched markets it's reversed: shorts +0.00R, longs +0.11R.
  • Why the training missed it: it checked each change on later years of the same markets. A habit of those particular markets passes that test. It needs checking on different markets too.
  • The honest best is about +1% a year at 1% risk, far from Fairtree. Raising the risk raises the swings as much as the profit.
