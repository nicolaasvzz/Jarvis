What changed to get here

  • Strong-break rule (18 Nov): the breakdown candle must be at least 1.2 ATR. It turned the video rules from losing to positive.
  • Daily stop-run within 20 days (18 Nov): the first positive result on fresh markets.
  • Real overnight costs (19 Nov): about −0.04R a trade, now always counted.
  • Shorts only (19 Nov): the edge was all in shorts; longs lost money on every test.
  • Trigger swing size 4 (19 Nov): only clear hourly turning points count.
  • Kept from the videos and confirmed again: pullback entry, fresh zones only, old-low targets.
